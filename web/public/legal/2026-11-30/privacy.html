CMA Privacy Notice

ChronoAI Pte. Ltd. in Singapore operates ChronoAI Managed Agent (CMA). This notice explains what CMA handles when you sign in, run Agents, or connect external services.

What we handle and why

  • Account and sign-in information. CMA uses NyxID for sign-in and service authorisation. CMA uses your account identifier, profile information supplied at sign-in, and session information to keep you signed in, recognise your account and check your access. It records the document version and time when you accept the service terms.
  • Sandbox and Agent content. CMA handles files, repositories, uploaded material, prompts, Agent replies, conversation history, and other data you put in a Sandbox. It uses this information to run your tasks, keep your workspace available where supported, and show your results. You can choose to share certain items or to send information to a connected service.
  • Operational and usage information. CMA records service events, request and diagnostic information, task outcomes, and resource or model usage. It uses these records to operate and secure CMA, troubleshoot faults, enforce limits and account for usage.

Service providers and processing locations

NyxID supplies identity and access services. Google Cloud provides hosting and storage, with Singapore and Tokyo as the planned CMA service regions. CMA uses MongoDB Atlas for managed database storage. The platform model gateway sends the instructions and context needed for a task to the applicable AI model provider. External services you choose to connect receive the information needed for the actions you authorise.

Retention and deletion

While your account is active, CMA keeps the information needed to run it. A paused Sandbox can still retain its files and Agent history. If you request account deletion, CMA's deletion process removes the account and its associated data. After deletion, CMA keeps a minimal record of acceptance consisting of the terms version, acceptance time and an anonymised account identifier. CMA keeps this record to document acceptance.

Access, correction and export

Under Singapore's PDPA, you can request access to and correction of your personal data, subject to applicable exceptions. You can withdraw consent for relevant uses of personal data. We will explain how withdrawal may affect the services you can use. We may verify your identity before acting on a request.

Where available, use Export account data in the account menu to download a retained conversation for an Agent you own. Workspace and saved-document exports use separate controls. Contact us for an access, correction, consent withdrawal or deletion request.
